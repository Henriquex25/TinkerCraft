# Community conduct

Be respectful, clear, and patient. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique code and ideas rather than people, and respect the maintainer's scope and available time.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, spam, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Share only code, images, and other material that you have permission to share.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation using GitHub's moderation tools. Responses should reflect the behavior and its impact.

For abusive content or privacy violations, use GitHub's **Report content** option where available. Do not repeat sensitive material in a public report; arrange a private channel with the maintainer when necessary. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
